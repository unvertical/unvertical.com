## 2. What personal data we collect

We only collect personal data that you voluntarily submit to us through the contact form on this website. The form collects:

- your **name**;
- your **email address**;
- your **company or organization** (optional);
- the **subject area** of your inquiry (selected from a list);
- the **content of your message**.

We do **not** use cookies, web analytics, advertising trackers, retargeting pixels, or social-media trackers. We do not collect any personal data passively from visitors who simply browse the site.

## 3. Why we process your data, and on what legal basis

We process the data described in section 2 solely to read your inquiry, evaluate it, and respond to it.

The legal basis is:

- **Article 6(1)(b) GDPR** — processing necessary to take steps at your request prior to entering into a contract — when your inquiry concerns our products or services; or
- **Article 6(1)(f) GDPR** — our legitimate interest in responding to correspondence addressed to us — for any other inquiry.

We do not use your data for marketing, profiling, or any purpose other than handling your inquiry.

## 4. Form processor — Formspree

Form submissions are routed through **Formspree**, a service provided by Formspree, Inc. (United States), which acts as our data processor. Formspree receives the data you submit through the form, stores it on our behalf, and forwards it to us by email.

Formspree may retain submissions on its infrastructure in accordance with its own terms and privacy policy: <https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/>.

Because Formspree is established in the United States, your data is transferred outside the European Economic Area. Such transfers are protected by the **EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework** and/or by the **Standard Contractual Clauses** adopted by the European Commission, as applicable.

If a submission leads to an ongoing business relationship, related correspondence may be retained for the duration of that relationship and for any further period required by Polish accounting and commercial law.

## 8. Your rights under the GDPR

You have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR);
- have inaccurate or incomplete data **rectified** (Art. 16);
- have your data **erased** — the "right to be forgotten" (Art. 17);
- **restrict** processing (Art. 18);
- **object** to processing based on our legitimate interests (Art. 21);
- request **data portability** (Art. 20), where applicable;
- **withdraw consent** at any time, where consent is the legal basis (Art. 7(3)), without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal;
- **lodge a complaint** with a supervisory authority (Art. 77). In Poland, this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (*Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych*, "**UODO**"), <https://uodo.gov.pl/>.

To exercise any of these rights, write to **privacy@unvertical.com**. We will respond within one month of receiving your request, as required by Article 12(3) GDPR.

## 9. Automated decision-making

We do not carry out automated decision-making, including profiling, within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR.
